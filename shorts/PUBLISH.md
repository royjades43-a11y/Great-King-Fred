# PesaBrain Shorts batch 1 — publishing kit

Each file in `renders/` is a finished 1080×1920 Short, 43–46 s.

## Audio speed: what was done and why
- Your recordings ran 48–55 s at about 165–180 words a minute, with **8–11 s of pauses** in each.
- I **cut every pause longer than 0.2 s** down to a short breath, and **sped the voice up 5%** (pitch kept natural).
  Each Short got 5–9 s shorter, with no words lost.
- Final pace is about **185–190 words a minute** with almost no dead air. Commonly cited Shorts guidance puts narration at
  roughly 150–180 wpm, and older listening research found people comfortably follow speech up to around 200 wpm. Ours sits
  just over the common range on purpose, because the cut pauses do most of the work. Going much faster (1.2× and up)
  starts to sound robotic with an AI voice and makes the burned-in captions hard to read.
- Loudness is normalized to −14 LUFS, YouTube's playback level.
- Treat this as a starting point: compare the retention graphs in YouTube Studio after a week.

## Posting tips
- **One a day**, not all six at once, so each gets its own test audience. Suggested order: 1 → 4 → 5 → 2 → 3 → 6
  (strongest hooks first).
- **Link a related video.** In YouTube Studio, each Short can link to a long video. Short 1, 2, 3 and 5 → *The $5 Coffee Myth*; Short 4 → *The Boring Way to Get Rich*; Short 6 → *401(k), IRA or Roth?*
- **Reply to early comments** in the first hour; it's the strongest signal you control.
- **Check on a real phone** that nothing important hides under the buttons before posting the rest.
