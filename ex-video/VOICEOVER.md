# Video 10 voiceover

- **File:** `assets/voiceover.mp3` (9:07), generated by the channel owner in ElevenLabs from
  `scripts/10-back-to-your-ex-voiceover.txt` in two parts:
  - **Part 1:** the hook and the brain section, 2:23.
  - **Part 2:** everything from "So the pain is real", 6:44.
- **Joined** with a 0.6 s pause at the section break. Otherwise the audio is used as is; no pauses were cut.
- **One edit:** the voice added a word that isn't in the script ("**Also,** which habit should we cover next?").
  That single word and its short pause (543.76–544.40 s) were cut, so the closing line is exactly the
  channel's. The uncut join is kept locally as `.work/voiceover-joined-uncut.mp3`.
- **Mix:** `tools/mix.py` brings the voice to the channel's usual speech level, adds the piano bed and SFX, and
  masters to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.
