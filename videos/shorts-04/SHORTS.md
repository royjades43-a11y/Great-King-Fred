**Title:** They liked you more than you think 😳

**Description:**
```
Do people like you more than you think? In a 2018 study, strangers chatted for five minutes, then guessed how much their partner liked them. They consistently guessed too low. Researchers call it the "liking gap".

It lasted for months among new college roommates, and the shyer the person, the bigger the gap. That "that was awkward" feeling is often wrong.

**Title:** The man who named small talk 🏝️

**Description:**
```
Where does small talk come from? Anthropologist Bronisław Malinowski was technically an "enemy alien" when World War One broke out, so he spent years on the Trobriand Islands recording everything people said. In 1923 he named small talk: "phatic communion", bonding through words.

His point: small talk isn't about what you say. It's how two people say "I'm not a threat."
