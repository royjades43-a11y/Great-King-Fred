**Title:** Same woman. Different bridge. 🌉

**Description:**
```
In 1974, psychologists Donald Dutton and Arthur Aron had an attractive woman stop men halfway across a swaying suspension bridge, 70 meters above the rapids. She gave each man her number. Then they repeated it on a low, solid bridge.

From the solid bridge, 2 men out of 16 called. From the swaying bridge, 9 out of 18. Same woman. Same questions. The only difference was the bridge.
```
