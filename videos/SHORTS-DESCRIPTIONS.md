# SEO descriptions: 7 Shorts

How every description is built:
- **Primary keyword in the first sentence** (that's what search and the "more" preview read first),
  plus 2–3 natural variations further down. No keyword lists stuffed into sentences.
- **First two lines = the hook**: a viewer reading only the preview still gets the point.
- **Link line to the full video** (also set it as the Short's *Related video*).
- **A question** to prompt comments.
- **Exactly 3 hashtags** at the end. YouTube shows the first three above the title; more than that
  dilutes them.
- **Tags** (paste into the Tags field): exact phrase first, then variants.

Keywords were chosen from common search phrasing for this topic, not from live search-volume data.
Check them in vidIQ or YouTube search autocomplete before publishing and swap in any stronger
phrases you find.

## 02 · What a fight does to your body 😳
**Primary keyword:** emotional flooding

```
Emotional flooding is what happens to your body in an argument: your heart rate spikes, your thinking narrows, and your brain starts treating the conversation like a real threat. Relationship researcher Dr. John Gottman called it "flooding".

Once you're flooded, a calm conversation isn't really possible. That's why some people go silent: their nervous system takes talking off the table for a minute.
```
