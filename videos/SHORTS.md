## Why they're built this way

- **The hook is on screen from frame 0** (the big title on top). The first frame is the preview in
  the Shorts feed, and viewers decide within about a second.
- **Every Short opens on a strong spoken line**, not on "and" or "so". The cuts fall between
  sentences, with a short fade on the audio.
- **Word-by-word captions** (the current word in red), because many viewers watch Shorts muted. They
  sit above YouTube's bottom text area and clear of the right-hand like/comment buttons.
- **Same sage canvas**: the landscape picture sits on the identical background, so it reads as one
  frame, not a letterboxed clip.
- **Length 21–49 s**: short enough to be watched through and replayed, which counts more than raw
  view time on Shorts.
- **Questions in the pinned comments**, answerable in one word, to get the comments going.
