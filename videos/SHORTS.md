## Why they're built this way

- **Titles are short (≤ 40 characters)**, so they show in full over the video in the Shorts
  player. They lead with "why", "you" or "they" because those make it about the viewer.
- **Descriptions** give the payoff in one line, point to the related video, and carry 3–4 hashtags
  (YouTube shows only the first few).
- **Covers** repeat the hook in 2–3 big words with one red key word, the same stick figures and
  the sage background, so the seven sit together as a set in your Shorts grid.
- **The hook is also on screen from frame 0** of each Short, so the Short still has a strong preview
  if you pick a frame instead of uploading a cover.
