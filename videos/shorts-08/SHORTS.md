## 03 · `03-who-texted-last.mp4` (0:50)

**Title:** Stop counting who texted last 🧾

**Description:**
```
"If they cared, they'd text first." It feels like self-respect. But psychologists Margaret Clark and Judson Mills described two kinds of relationships: exchange relationships, where people keep score, and communal ones, like friendships, where people give because they care.

Counting who texted last quietly turns a friend into a transaction. And your friend may be keeping the same score. Both waiting. Neither one forgot.

## 07 · `07-how-to-text-first.mp4` (0:56)
**Cover:** `thumbnails/07-how-to-text-first.jpg`

**Title:** How to text first (without it being weird) ✅

**Description:**
```
5 rules for texting first:

1. Don't wait for a reason. "Saw this and thought of you" is enough.
2. Skip the apology. "It's been forever, I miss talking to you" beats a paragraph about being busy.
3. Say something real. Try "what's been the best part of your month?"
4. Stop keeping score. Texting first doesn't make you needy.
5. Make it a habit. One person a week. About 10 seconds.
