At 1:25 a.m. on March 26, 2024, the containership Dali lost electrical power about 3,200 feet from Baltimore's Francis Scott Key Bridge. Four minutes and nine seconds later it struck Pier 17, the central spans fell, and six highway workers on the bridge were killed. Using the NTSB's final report, we trace the blackout to a single loose wire, then ask why the 47-year-old bridge was never assessed for ship-strike risk and why the crew on it was never warned.
