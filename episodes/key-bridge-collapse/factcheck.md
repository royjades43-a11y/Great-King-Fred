# Fact check: One Loose Wire: Why the Key Bridge Fell

Checked against NTSB MIR-25-40 (final, cited at printed page; PDF page = printed + 2), MIR-25-10, the preliminary report, and the NTSB press releases of Nov 18, 2025 and Mar 20, 2025. Text copies of both releases are now saved as `research/reports/NTSB_PR_NR20251118.txt` and `NTSB_PR_nr20250320.txt`.

**Result:** 5 must-fix (all fixed in script.json and voiceover.txt), 8 should-fix, 12 notes. `node scripts/build_timeline.mjs episodes/key-bridge-collapse --estimate` passes (12:22 estimated, 84 scenes).
