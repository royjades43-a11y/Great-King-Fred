# Bible Stories in Stained Glass: story list

34 stories in six seasons, in the order the Bible tells them. Episode folders use the story
number (`17-david-and-goliath`). The order can be rearranged to follow a particular teaching
series; the numbers are just slots.

Each story has:
- **Key verse:** the King James Version reference for the verse written on the scroll at the end.
- **Signature moment:** the one effect the episode and its thumbnail are built around.
- **New art:** glass sets and figures still to be drawn.

## Writing each episode

- **Length:** 4–6 minutes, about 600–850 words.
- **Shape:** a hook (one striking image or question), the story in 3–5 scenes, then the key verse written on the scroll, and one closing line that turns the verse into the lesson.
- **Faithfulness:** narration paraphrases the passage closely and doesn't add events. Traditions or interpretations that aren't in the text are introduced as such ("tradition says…"). The verse card quotes the KJV, which is public domain.
- **Sources:** each `episode.yaml` starts with a comment listing the verses each scene draws on, as in episode 17.
