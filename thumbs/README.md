# PesaBrain thumbnails, v2 (character-led)

Each thumbnail has **one big relatable character** on the right and **2–5 words** on the left. Exactly **one word is highlighted** in a terra-red box with white letters.
Designed at 1920×1080 and exported as 1280×720 JPG to `out/`. `out/all-thumbnails-preview.jpg` shows all 15.

**House look (the same for every video):**
- Background: paper `#F4EBD5` with a soft vignette.
- Ink outline: `#231F1C`, 12 px at 1920 scale, with round caps.
- Highlight box: red `#C4553F` with white `#FFFDF7` letters.
- Fills: mustard `#E9C25B`, olive `#8E9B6A`, slate `#5F7C90`, pink `#E7958C`, terra `#B8704F`.
- Font: Archivo Black (thumbnail text is 140–150 px tall on the 1920 canvas).
- Bottom-right corner is kept clear for YouTube's timestamp.

**Rebuild:** `node thumbs/build.mjs`, or `node thumbs/build.mjs 03` for one video. The characters live in `thumbs/chars.mjs`.

**How to test:** Upload **A** as the main thumbnail and put **B and C** in YouTube's Test & Compare. A is always the relatable moment, B a surprising fact, C the promise of a fix.
