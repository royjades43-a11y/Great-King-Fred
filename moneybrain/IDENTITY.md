# MONEY BRAIN — channel identity

**What the channel is:** the psychology of money, explained by three characters. It shares the muted, warm palette of
stickman explainers but nothing else. There's no round-headed stickman, no marker-caps title across the top and no plain cream wall.

## The cast

| Character | Who they are | How to recognise them | Job in every episode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bean** | You, the viewer | A golden jelly-bean body with no separate head, a two-leaf sprout on top, big oval eyes and terracotta sneakers | Lives the money moment and makes the choices |
| **Impulse** | Your brain | A pink brain on legs, sunglasses pushed up, half-lidded eyes, a toothy grin and white sneakers | The voice that says "buy it", "only $5", "just pay the minimum" |
| **Prof. Calc** | The math | A walking slate-blue calculator: its screen shows the number, round glasses, a mustache, a button-grid belly | Runs the real numbers |

Bean, Impulse and Prof. Calc are names for the channel bible. The narration always says "you" and "your brain".

## The world
- **Ledger paper:** cream paper with faint ruled lines and a double terracotta margin line on the left. Characters stand on the darker rule.
- **Palette:** paper `#F4EBD5` · ink `#231F1C` · terracotta `#B8704F` · olive `#8E9B6A` · slate `#5F7C90` · mustard `#D3A43F` · Bean gold `#E2B04A` · Impulse pink `#E7958C` · alert red `#C4553F`.
- **Line:** a 5 px ink outline on everything, with a subtle "boil" (the lines redraw 8 times a second), so stills feel hand-drawn and alive.

## Recurring devices (the signature)
- **Price-tag titles:** each idea's key words go on a swinging price tag, set in Archivo Black. The colour says the mood:
  white for neutral, mustard for "reasons and steps", olive for good moves, terracotta or red for costs and warnings, slate for data, ink for dramatic beats.
- **Receipts for numbers:** money figures print out of a slot like a till receipt and count up, with typing and chime sounds.
- **Rubber-stamp chapters:** PART 1 THE CAR · PART 2 INTEREST · PART 3 LIFESTYLE CREEP · PART 4 FREE MONEY · THE TWIST · YOUR PLAN, each slammed on with a bass hit and a small camera shake.
- **Verdict stamps:** MISLEADING, NOT GUILTY, TOO LATE.
- **Motion:** parts pop in on the word that names them, the camera pushes in slowly through each scene, and the characters breathe slightly.

## Type
