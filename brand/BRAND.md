# The Great Window: brand guide

**Name:** The Great Window
**Tagline:** *Every Bible story is one pane of a single window.*
**Promise:** Bible stories told in living stained glass, then read like a window: what the story says, what it teaches, and where it points.

Everything here is generated by `lantern/brand.py` from the same glass engine as the videos.

## Profile picture: `avatar.png` (800×800)

A rose window of eight jewel-coloured petals around a glowing gold centre with a cross of light. It reads at 98 px as "a bright stained-glass circle", and it's the same rose the episodes fill, pane by pane.

## Banner: `banner.png` (2560×1440)

Seven lancet windows, with the rose in the tallest middle one. The others hold story panes (David, Samson) and symbols (the ark, the tablets, the star, the fish). The name and tagline sit beside them in gold. Everything important is inside YouTube's mobile-safe area (the centre 1546×423), so nothing gets cropped on phones. Regenerate it as more panes are finished: `python -m lantern.brand banner -o brand/banner.png`.

## Thumbnails (1280×720)

1. **One frozen moment.** Use the episode's signature moment at its peak: the temple mid-collapse, the stone mid-flight. Push in so the subject fills the right two-thirds (`--focus x,y --zoom 1.5–2`).
2. **Two to three gold words** in heavy serif on the darkened left, posed as a question the video answers: *ONLY THIS ONCE*, *THE LAST VOW*, *ONE STONE*. Never the story's title, because the video title already says that.
3. **The numbered roundel** top right: the story's number in a small rose. It tells viewers this is one pane of a set, which encourages them to collect the rest.
4. **Keep the stone arch** visible at the edges. It brands the thumbnail before the viewer reads a word.

```bash
python -m lantern.brand thumb episodes/bible/14-samson --at 268.3 --text "ONLY|THIS ONCE" --number 14 \
    -o brand/thumb_14_samson_a.png
```

Make two thumbnails per episode and A/B test them with YouTube's "Test & compare".

## Titles

Story name first, then the hook: **"Samson: The Vow He Broke First"**, **"David and Goliath: The Battle Was Never His"**.

## Colours

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Gold text and lines | `#F2CF6E` |
| Stone | `#423C36` |
| Lead | `#1E1B1A` |
| Glass | sky `#3460AA`, ruby `#A82228`, emerald `#2E804A`, gold `#DEA836`, violet `#623E8C` |

**Type:** a heavy classical serif for words (Liberation Serif Bold here; Cinzel or Trajan suit it if you design by hand), and italic serif for taglines and labels.
