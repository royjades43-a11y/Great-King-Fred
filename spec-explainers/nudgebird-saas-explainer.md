# Spec Explainer: **Nudgebird** (fictional B2B SaaS)

> Portfolio spec piece. Nudgebird is a made-up product, so no client approval is needed.
> **Length:** about 90s (main cut) plus a 15s social cutdown
> **Format:** 16:9 master, 9:16 reframe for the cutdown
> **Style:** high-energy motion animation with bold shapes, snappy easing, a character-driven metaphor, and kinetic type for key phrases

---

## The product (for context)
Nudgebird plugs into Slack, Jira, Asana, email, and the calendar. It spots tasks that have quietly stalled, such as unanswered approvals, forgotten action items, and tickets nobody owns. Then it sends the right person a friendly nudge before the delay becomes a problem.

**Target buyer:** Ops leads and team managers at 50–500 person companies.
**Core promise:** "Nothing falls through the cracks."

## The central visual metaphor
Work is shown as **glowing paper planes** flying between people. A stalled task is a plane that **loses lift, wobbles, and drifts toward a crack in the floor.** Nudgebird is a small, sharp, slightly smug **bird** who swoops in and taps the plane back into the air.

The bird is the brand mascot. Keep it expressive but minimal: two shapes and an eye.

## Production Notes

- **Pacing:** Shots 1–5 are the problem (35s), which feels long but does the selling. Viewers have to *feel* the dropped ball before the bird shows up. The silent beat in shot 5 is deliberate, so don't cut it.
- **Motion language:**
  - Healthy work uses smooth, confident arcs and an ease-out.
  - Stalled work uses jittery, decaying motion and a desaturated color.
  - Nudgebird moves with a sharp ease-in-out and overshoot, quick and precise.
- **Palette:** Deep navy background, warm glowing planes in amber and white, the crack in dark red, and Nudgebird in one bright accent color (electric teal) so the bird always pops.
- **Music:** Minimal pulse for 0–20s, cut out for 20–35s, then a driving upbeat track from the reveal onward.
- **Real logos:** Use generic tool shapes, not real Slack/Jira/etc. marks, which keeps the spec piece clean legally.
- **Stats:** Mark them as illustrative in the portfolio description. Swap in real ones for a real client.

## How to Reuse This as a Template for Clients
The structure works for almost any B2B SaaS:

1. **Show the normal flow** (5–12s): what work looks like when it's fine.
2. **One thing breaks** (12–35s): make the pain visual and personal.
3. **Hero reveal** (35–42s): the product as a character or force.
4. **Rewind and fix** (42–52s): replay the same moment with the product in it.
5. **3-step how-it-works** (52–62s): Connect / Detect / Act style.
6. **Zoom out and payoff** (62–80s): the whole system healthy, plus proof.
7. **Tagline and CTA** (80–90s).

Swap out the metaphor (planes and crack) for whatever matches the client's problem, and the rest of the beats stay the same.
