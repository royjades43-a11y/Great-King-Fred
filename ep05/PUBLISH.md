The numbers: $6,000 at 22% APR. Minimum payment modelled as interest plus 1% of the balance (about $170 at the start). Minimum-only: 20+ years and about $15,900 in total. A fixed $170: just under 5 years and about $9,750. Avalanche vs. snowball example: three cards ($800 at 15%, $2,700 at 27%, $2,500 at 20%), $300 a month. Your card's formula may differ; your statement's minimum-payment warning box shows your own numbers.
