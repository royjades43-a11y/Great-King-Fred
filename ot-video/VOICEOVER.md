# Video 12 voiceover

- **File:** `assets/voiceover.mp3` (9:19), generated by the channel owner in ElevenLabs in one take from
  `scripts/12-overthink-voiceover.txt`.
- **No edits:** the audio is used as is, with no pauses cut.
- **Mix:** `tools/mix.py` brings the voice to the channel's usual speech level, adds the piano bed and SFX, and
  masters to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.

## Verification

Whisper transcribed the file and matched it to the script word by word (`timing/heard.json`). Every sentence is
present and in order, and all 1,478 words are timed.

Seven spots looked off in the first pass and were re-checked by slowing the audio down. In each, the voice reads
the script; the small Whisper model misheard words that sound alike:
- "And they're asleep." (first heard as "there is sleep")
- "They've pinged thousands of people" (first heard as "pained")
- "the wearers guessed" (heard as "wearer's guest")
- "a cow chews its cud" (heard as "chooses its cut")
- "Some just picked the one they liked" (heard as "pick the one")
- "psychologists E. J. Masicampo" and "So here's what you can do" (dropped words that are present when slowed)

Other differences are spelling only: digits for spoken numbers, "okay" for "ok", and names spelled differently
("Gilevich", "Manelow", "Lubamirski", "Zygonic", "Balmwaister", "Cross").
