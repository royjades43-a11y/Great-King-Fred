# Original Short 04 · Same ticket (the psychology of money)

**Hook on screen (first 2 s):** "Same ticket. $29 or $61?"
**Length:** 92 words, about 33 seconds at Josh's pace.

## Why this should travel (applied research)

Shorts guides agree on a few things (vendor sources, so treat the numbers loosely, but the advice is
consistent):
- the first 1–2 seconds decide whether viewers swipe ([ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm-decoded.html))
- an ending that flows back into the opening invites replays ([Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/youtube-shorts-hooks-and-curiosity-loops-that-explode-your-views/))
- the retention graph, not how clever the hook sounded, is the judge ([JoinBrands](https://joinbrands.com/blog/youtube-shorts-best-practices/))

Applied here:
- **Hook in under 2 seconds with a concrete gap:** "Same ticket." plus two prices. The viewer has to know why.
- **The question is answered in one line ("How they paid."), then proven,** so the payoff comes early and the
  viewer stays for the "why".
- **A second reveal halfway through** (brain scans) re-hooks the viewer right where Shorts usually lose people.
- **Money is universal:** everyone has tapped a card this week, so the takeaway is something they can use today.
- **A loop ending:** the last words, "it's the same ticket", run straight back into the first words, "Same
  ticket." A replay feels seamless.
- **A visual change every 1–2 seconds,** and the hook text holds for the feed preview.

## Voiceover (paste into ElevenLabs)

```
Same ticket. One group bid twenty-nine dollars. The other bid sixty-one. The only difference? How they paid. At MIT, students bid on tickets to a sold-out Celtics game. Half were told they'd pay in cash. Half, by credit card. The card group bid more than double. Twenty years later, brain scans showed why. When people shopped with a card, the brain's reward center lit up. With cash, it didn't. To your brain, the card itself starts to feel like a treat. So next time you tap, ask one question. Would I pay this in cash? Because it's the same ticket.
```

Voice: Josh, same settings as the long videos. Read it with energy, but don't rush the first two words.
"Same ticket." needs a beat after it.
