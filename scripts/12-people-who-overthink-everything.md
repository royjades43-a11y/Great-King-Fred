# Video 12: The Psychology of People Who Overthink Everything

**Title:** The Psychology of People Who Overthink Everything
**Length:** 1,478 words. That's about 9:00 at the pace of video 11's voice, so it's over 8:00 and mid-roll ads are possible.

**How it differs from competitors:** most videos on this topic are "10 signs you're an overthinker" or
"just stop overthinking". This one shows what researchers measured: an app that pinged thousands of people to
ask where their minds were, students in a Barry Manilow T-shirt, a worry diary checked against reality, a free
poster that people liked less after thinking about it, and a plan that quiets a thought without doing the task.
It lands kindly. Overthinkers are often the most careful people you know. Thinking isn't the problem; thinking
in circles is.

**Engagement design:**
- **Talks straight to the viewer throughout:** "sound familiar?", "that conversation you've been replaying all week",
  "how much of your week have you spent on something that never happened?".
- **Opens on the viewer's own moment:** a one-word "ok." text read at midnight, forty minutes of spiralling, and
  the other person asleep.
- **An open loop paid off later:** "the one simple move that helps it let go" is answered at about 6:30 (write a
  specific plan), then again in rule one.
- **A running image:** the "ok." text. It starts the hook, comes back with "the text, the meeting", and closes the
  video ("they're probably just busy, or asleep").
- **Irony and human detail:**
  - "And they're asleep."
  - everyone else is busy feeling like they're on stage too
  - rumination is named after a cow chewing its cud
  - the maximizers "did better, and felt worse"
  - "your worry has a terrible track record"
  - the mind that replays an awkward text also remembers your best friend's coffee order
- **A rhetorical question about every 90 seconds**, and "But it wasn't over yet." at the end of section 5.

Numbers and years are written the way they are spoken, so `scripts/12-overthink-voiceover.txt` can be pasted
straight into ElevenLabs. The reading guide is `scripts/12-overthink-voice-direction.md`. Lines in *[brackets]*
are visual notes for the edit; they are not read out.

**Visual style:** same as videos 9 to 11. No chapter cards. One text zone at the top with short phrases, never
full captions. The stage below is for stick figures and drawings, with gentle motion timed to the words.
