# PesaBrain EP06 — "How Much Money Should You Keep in the Bank?"

A 9:17 HyperFrames episode built on your voiceover: 138 drawn scenes, each starting on the exact word it illustrates.

- `renders/rainy-day-web.mp4` — the video (1920×1080, 30 fps)
- `SCRIPT.md` — the script with fact notes · `voiceover-*.txt` — the plain-text version for ElevenLabs
- `PUBLISH.md` — titles, thumbnails, ad breaks, description with chapters and sources, tags, pinned comment
- `BEATS.md` — every scene's start time, tag and the words it starts on

## This episode's own personality: the weather
- **Emergencies are storm clouds,** labelled CAR, SICK, JOB, LAYOFF, and they arrive on a lifetime timeline.
- **The fund is an umbrella that grows:** small at Level 1 ($1K), bigger at Level 2 (1 month), widest at Level 3 (3–6 months).
- **A brick wall** labelled FUND stands between Bean and the EP05 debt trap.
- **The fund tank** with three marks fills, empties and refills.
- **Named accounts** ("EMERGENCY ONLY", "CAR BREAKS", "DO NOT TOUCH"), labelled envelopes and a padlock for "out of sight".
- **The bookends:** the car's "clunk!" and the $1,200 clipboard at the start, and the same scene two years later, when Bean doesn't flinch.

Episode props are in `tools/props6.mjs`; it reuses EP02's cushion, EP03's hill, snowball and hammock, EP04's boxes and trap, and EP05's anchor and statement.
