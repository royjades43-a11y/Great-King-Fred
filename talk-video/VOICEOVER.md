## Performance direction (new in video 13)

- **The host:** one stick figure speaks the narration to the viewer. Its mouth opens on every spoken word (the `talk`
  option in `tools/build.mjs`, synced to the audio).
- **Posture performs the line:** the host gestures through a vocabulary added to `tools/stick.mjs`.
  - explain: open palms
  - present / presentL: offering what's on stage
  - chin: thinking
  - ear: asking the viewer
  - lean: before a personal question
  - count: one finger
  - embarrassed, search, handsHips, wave
- **Story figures act the studies:** the child with blocks, the neighbour who walks in, Hurlburt with the beeper,
  the tennis player and runner, the two speakers, the friend who drops a glass. The host watches, reacts, then turns
  back to the viewer.
- **Places:** soft muted line drawings behind each chapter (`bgPlayroom`, `bgKitchen`, `bgStudy`, `bgDesk`,
  `bgCourt`, `bgStage`).
- **The ending returns to the playroom:** the four-year-old is back, a callback to the opening.
- **Tone:** calm and warm. Expressions change with the meaning (smile, grin, neutral, "o", tight), never with
  big drama.
