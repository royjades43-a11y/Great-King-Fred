# Video 13 voiceover

- **File:** `assets/voiceover.mp3` (9:03), generated by the channel owner in ElevenLabs from
  `scripts/13-talk-to-themselves-voiceover.txt`.
- **Check:** Whisper matched the script in order. The differences are names and spoken numbers written as digits
  ("Vigotsky", "Lupian", "Herbert", "Netagard", "1930s", "2012"). Four spots looked off and were re-checked slowed
  down; each is a sound-alike mishearing by the small model:
  - "inner speech" (heard as "inner speed")
  - "your self-talk" (heard as "yourself taught")
  - "grew up" (heard as "grow up")
  - "should we cover" (heard as "should recover")
- **No edits:** the audio is used as is.

## Performance direction (new in video 13)

- **The host:** one stick figure speaks the narration to the viewer. Its mouth opens on every spoken word (the `talk`
  option in `tools/build.mjs`, synced to the audio).
- **Posture performs the line:** the host gestures through a vocabulary added to `tools/stick.mjs`.
  - explain: open palms
  - present / presentL: offering what's on stage
  - chin: thinking
  - ear: asking the viewer
  - lean: before a personal question
  - count: one finger
  - embarrassed, search, handsHips, wave
- **Story figures act the studies:** the child with blocks, the neighbour who walks in, Hurlburt with the beeper,
  the tennis player and runner, the two speakers, the friend who drops a glass. The host watches, reacts, then turns
  back to the viewer.
- **Places:** soft muted line drawings behind each chapter (`bgPlayroom`, `bgKitchen`, `bgStudy`, `bgDesk`,
  `bgCourt`, `bgStage`).
- **The ending returns to the playroom:** the four-year-old is back, a callback to the opening.
- **Tone:** calm and warm. Expressions change with the meaning (smile, grin, neutral, "o", tight), never with
  big drama.

## Render note

At 4K a single worker runs about 0.6 s per frame (~2.7 h for 9 min), and multi-worker renders need ~136 GB of temporary frames. So the
master is rendered in scene parts: `python3 tools/mkparts.py` writes one root file per scene on exact frame boundaries,
`tools/render-parts.sh` renders them four at a time (about 50 min in total), and ffmpeg joins them with `assets/soundtrack.m4a` at CRF 19.
