# Video 11 voiceover

- **File:** `assets/voiceover.mp3` (9:20), generated by the channel owner in ElevenLabs in one take from
  `scripts/11-cant-say-no-voiceover.txt`.
- **No edits:** the audio is used as is, with no pauses cut.
- **Mix:** `tools/mix.py` brings the voice to the channel's usual speech level, adds the piano bed and SFX, and
  masters to -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.

## Verification

Whisper transcribed the file in pause-cut clips and matched it to the script word by word (`timing/heard.json`).
Every sentence is present and in order, and all 1,537 words are timed.

Three spots looked off in the first pass and were re-checked by slowing the audio down. In each, the voice reads
the script exactly; the first pass was a mishearing by the small Whisper model:
- "they were offered a thank-you snack" (first heard as "they will offer")
- "'I don't' refusals persuaded other people too" (first heard as "I don't refuse")
- "Try, 'Let me check…'" (first heard as "Try it")

Other differences are spelling only: digits for spoken numbers, and names spelled differently ("Balmeister",
"Bones", "Gals Albumen", "Balino", "Hocktvedt").
